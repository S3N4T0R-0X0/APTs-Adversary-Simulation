Aircraft Systems Design Manager

California 29 September 2024 $205000 / year
Full Time
Application ends: 6 February 2025

Job Description

We are looking for a senior engineer, to assist the company president/chief engineer with implementing and managing multiple program opportunities, from definition through certification. Current
engineering staff is fully capable of handling component design and installation, but critical need exists for engineering manager with total system integration experience. The attributes that we are looking
for are:

Technical Skills

  • Broad experience in architecting business class jet, mechanical systems and controls.
  • Mechanical, hydraulic and pneumatic systems.
  • Design of aircraft system installations.
  • Cockpit controls, system logic, simple DC electrical circuits.
  • Designing and Setting up system bench tests.
  • Calculating system performance and component sizing.
  • Drafting component specifications.
  • Flight test engineering, planning and data acquisition systems.
  • Writing certification reports, including System Safety Assessments.

Business Management Skills

  • Program Scheduling and Resource Planning
  • Business Plans and Proposals
  • Program Cost Estimating
  • Supplier negotiations and management
  • Professional Presentations
  • Aircraft Market Studies
